Your Privacy Rights

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

At SlyD, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Email address, encrypted password, profile name, age, and optional dating profile preferences (such as role, relationship status, bio, and tribes).
  • Photos & Media: Profile photos and private album images uploaded by you.
  • Geolocation Data: With your explicit consent, we process approximate latitude and longitude coordinates to calculate distances between nearby members on the grid and map.
  • AI Verification Photos: If you use our Anti-Catfish selfie verification feature, selfie images are analyzed via Gemini AI to confirm likeness against your profile photos. Selfies submitted strictly for verification are not published publicly to your profile.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide discovery matching, deliver secure messaging, prevent fraud and impersonation, process premium subscriptions via Stripe, and ensure community safety.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties or advertisers. We share information only with trusted service infrastructure providers necessary to operate the application (such as Google Firebase for database storage and Stripe for payment processing).

4. Incognito Mode & Privacy Controls

SlyD gives you control over your visibility:

  • You can toggle off "Show Distance" or "Show Online Status" in App Preferences.
  • SlyD+ members can activate Incognito Mode to browse profiles without appearing in the nearby grid or active radars.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. When you delete your account, your profile data, uploaded media, and message records are permanently deleted from our active database.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please email us at privacy@slyd.online.
